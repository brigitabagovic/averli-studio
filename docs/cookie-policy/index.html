What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in comparable ways. Some are needed for a site to work. Others, such as analytics, are optional and need your consent.

Managing your choice

  • Accept or reject: use the cookie choice shown on your first visit. “Accept” and “Reject” are equally easy.
  • Change your mind or withdraw consent: use “Cookie settings” in the footer at any time. Withdrawing consent also removes the analytics cookies where the browser allows it.
  • Browser controls: you can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
