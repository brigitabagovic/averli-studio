What we collect and why

Contact form (project inquiries)

If you use the form on the Contact page we collect your name, email address, your brand or business (optional), the service you are interested in and your message. We use them only to read and reply to your inquiry and, if it leads to a project, to prepare a proposal and work with you. Your browser sends the form to a relay we run on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers, forms.averlistudio.com), which passes it to our email provider Resend and then to our business mailbox. The relay does not keep a copy. For abuse protection it keeps a short-lived counter of requests per IP address (about 10 minutes).

Email and WhatsApp

If you email info@averlistudio.com or message us on WhatsApp, we receive your contact details and whatever you send, and use them to reply.

Website statistics (only with your consent)

If you click “Accept” in the cookie choice, we use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are viewed, where visitors come from (campaign tags in the link you used) and whether the contact form was completed (together with the service category chosen in it) or the email or WhatsApp link was clicked. Page addresses are sent without any query other than campaign tags and without the part after #, the referrer is sent without its query, and events carry no name, email or message text. Google receives your IP address and browser details as part of the request. We have switched off Google signals and ad personalisation. If you reject, none of this runs and nothing is sent to Google.

Technical data

The website is a static site on GitHub Pages. Like any host, the provider processes technical data needed to deliver the pages, such as your IP address, the time of the request and browser details. We do not use this to identify you.

Legal bases

  • Replying to your inquiry and preparing a proposal: steps at your request before a contract and our legitimate interest in answering business inquiries (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)).
  • Website statistics (Google Analytics 4): your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), given in the cookie choice and withdrawable at any time.
  • Security, abuse protection and keeping the site available: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Legal and accounting obligations, if a project goes ahead: Art. 6(1)(c).

Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable format and to withdraw consent at any time. To use these rights email info@averlistudio.com. You can change your cookie choice with “Cookie settings” in the footer.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. In Croatia this is the Personal Data Protection Agency (Agencija za zaštitu osobnih podataka, AZOP), azop.hr.
